How to Play
1
Gather Players
Minimum 2 players. The more the merrier!
2
Open a Card
A category appears — like "One Teacher" or "One Food Item".
Example: Name one teacher you are thankful for.
3
Everyone Shares
Each player names something from that category they are grateful for.
4
Tell the Story
Share why you are thankful — a memory, a reason, a feeling.
5
Next Card
Tap Next Card and keep the gratitude going!
😇 Goal of the Game
Pause, reflect, and share what you are truly grateful for. A beautiful way to connect and appreciate life together.
